STEP BY STEP 

This step-by-step roadmap is designed to move your project from a basic concept to a Proactive Educational Governance Framework. To achieve a Q1-level research impact, you must move beyond simple "pass/fail" predictions and focus on a hierarchical escalation and prescriptive intervention system.
________________________________________
Step 1: Framework Creation (The "State Machine" Logic)
The core novelty of your project is the Escalation Protocol. Instead of just a one-time prediction, the system acts as a persistent monitor.
1.	Level 1 (Advisor/Course Teacher): Triggered by early warning signs (e.g., two consecutive absences or a 20% drop in LMS engagement). Intervention: Automated email or 1-on-1 meeting.
2.	Level 2 (Counselor/Dean): Triggered if Level 1 interventions fail or if the risk score remains "High" for a full semester. Intervention: Psychological counseling or academic foundation plan.
3.	Level 3 (Academic VP): Triggered by critical persistent failure or imminent dropout risk. Intervention: Strategic financial aid review or mandatory program re-evaluation.
Step 2: Data Creation (The "Digital Twin" Approach)
Since real student data is private, you will build a Synthetic Data Generation Pipeline. This is a highly technical skill that reviewers love.
1.	Acquire a Seed Dataset: Use the OULAD (Open University Learning Analytics Dataset) which includes student logs, demographic data, and results.
2.	Augment with Synthetic Data: Use CTGAN (Conditional Tabular Generative Adversarial Network) to expand the dataset.
o	Feature Engineering: Create features for:
	Academic: Attendance, GPA, Submission Latency (on-time vs. late).
	Engagement: LMS login frequency, library entry logs.
	Socio-Financial: Program duration, club involvement, financial aid status.
3.	Temporal Longitudinal Labeling: Data must be labeled semester-by-semester to track the "Velocity of Risk."
Step 3: Model Development (Explainable & Fair AI)
A Q1 journal requires you to prove your model isn't biased and is interpretable for human decision-makers.
1.	Algorithm Selection: Use XGBoost for its superior handling of structured tabular student data.
2.	The XAI Layer (Interpretability): Integrate SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations). When a student is escalated to the Dean, the system must generate a "Risk Card" explaining why (e.g., "70% of risk is driven by late submissions").
3.	Fairness Auditing: Use the IBM AIF360 toolkit to ensure the AI doesn't unfairly target students based on non-academic factors like socioeconomic status.
Step 4: Results & Evaluation
For a top-tier journal, your results must be comprehensive:
•	Classification Performance: Report Accuracy, F1-score, and ROC-AUC. Target an F1-score of $>0.85$ to ensure reliability.
•	Escalation Success Rate: Measure how many "Level 1" students successfully moved back to "Low Risk" after the prescribed intervention.
•	Inference Latency: Prove the system can process thousands of student records in real-time (target $<2$ seconds per batch).
Industry Impact 
•	The Recommendation Engine: Don't just flag a student; provide a Prescriptive Solution. If the AI sees low math marks, it should automatically suggest a "Foundational Math" bridge course.
•	Real-time Dashboard: Build a Streamlit Dashboard where the Academic VP can see a "Heatmap of Risk" across the entire university, allowing for strategic resource allocation.
